<h2 class="wp-block-heading">About asipofcoffe </h2>



<p>A Sip of Coffee is a coffee blog for people who want a better cup — whether that means a cleaner pour-over on a slow morning, a balanced espresso shot at home, or a cold brew that finally tastes like the café version.</p>



<p>We're not here to make coffee complicated. We're here to make it repeatable. Every guide and recipe is written by someone who actually does the work — pulling shots, dialing in grinders, roasting small batches, and testing drinks until they're good enough to share.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What we cover</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Espresso &amp; milk drinks</strong> — technique you can repeat at home.</li>



<li><strong>Brewing methods</strong> — pour-over, French press, AeroPress, and the ratios that matter.</li>



<li><strong>Beans &amp; roasting</strong> — choosing, storing, and tasting coffee with more confidence.</li>



<li><strong>Iced &amp; specialty drinks</strong> — cold brew, flavored lattes, and seasonal favorites.</li>



<li><strong>Coffee &amp; wellness</strong> — clear, honest information about caffeine, decaf, and acidity.</li>



<li><strong>Hosting</strong> — serving coffee to guests and building a simple home coffee bar.</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why trust us</h2>



<p>We write from hands-on experience, not recycled tips. When a recipe needs an exact ratio, temperature, or grind size, we give it — along with the <em>why</em> behind it and the common mistake to avoid. Our writers each focus on the area they know best, and you can read their full backgrounds on our author pages.</p>



<p>We're also honest about limits. On health and caffeine topics we keep things educational, never medical, and we point you to a qualified professional for personal decisions. See our <a href="https://claude.ai/disclaimer/">Disclaimer</a> for details.</p>
